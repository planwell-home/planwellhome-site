Questions

What do I get?

Each product is a fillable, printable PDF plus a spreadsheet you can copy into Google Sheets or open in Excel. Everything is a digital download. Nothing is mailed.

Where can I buy?

Right here on this page, or on Etsy if you prefer. Prices and files are the same in both places. Prefer Etsy? We're there too.

Can I get a refund?

Because these are digital downloads, all sales are final, whether you buy here or on Etsy. If a file won't open or something looks wrong, email hello@planwellhome.com and we'll help.

Is this professional advice?

No. These templates are general education to help you plan and stay organized. They don't replace a contractor, home inspector, designer or other professional who can look at your home.

Do the rules where I live matter?

Yes. Permits, contractor rules and building requirements vary from place to place. Check with your state and your town, village, city or county before you start work.
